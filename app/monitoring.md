## Why are AME tools on Data Library?

Placing the AME Tools in a common repository ensures centralized access
by Global HQ, and Country Office colleagues, supports effective
oversight and granular analyses on AME budgeting and facilitates
effective collaboration on AME budgeting across teams.

## Why are the MMR Coverage Plan, MMR Waiver and Annual MMR Coverage Report on Data Library? 

Placing the key MMR-related documents in a common repository ensures
centralized access by Global HQ and CO colleagues, promotes consistency,
facilitates effective support for appropriately planning to meet the
MMRs and supports effective oversight of compliance with the MMRs.

More specifically, as part of oversight responsibilities, regional
offices are responsible for verifying CO's planned compliance with the
MMRs during the CSP design phase and annually as part of the IP
submission by reviewing the MMR Coverage Plan and MMR Waiver, if needed.
This contributes to ensuring that COs are planning to meet the MMRs and
provides quality assurance for CSP products. Regional offices should
also review CO's implementation of the MMRs, including assessments
implemented, actual process monitoring site visits and outcome
monitoring exercises implemented versus the MMRs for annual tracking and
reporting on MMR implementation; this information is found in the
'Annual MMR Coverage Report'.

Global HQ is responsible for tracking and reporting on global compliance
with the MMRs through the 2026 Management Plan Key Performance
Indicator, 'Percentage of COs that meet the MMRs' by reviewing Annual
MMR Coverage Reports.

## How should the monitoring documents be uploaded on Data Library by COs? 

Documents should be uploaded by 28 February each ear for ongoing CSPs.
Users can upload the documents in their Data Container by creating a New
Record and adding the appropriate metadata in the Category and Type
metadata.

## What access level should be set for the monitoring documents? 

WFP: Only users with a WFP account can download the data
